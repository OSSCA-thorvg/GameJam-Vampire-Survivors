Modifications
  hud.ttf is a Modified Version under the OFL: the Regular instance (wght 400, wdth 100) was cut from the variable
  font, and the glyph set reduced to U+0020-007E with layout tables, STAT and hinting removed. The font names and the
  copyright and license name records are kept. The copyright statement declares no Reserved Font Name. Rebuild with
  scripts/make-hud-font.sh (fonttools 4.68.0), which records the exact commands.
